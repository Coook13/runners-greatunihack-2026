# Runners: 3-minute demo script

Two speakers. Times match the beats in `src/demo/director.ts` (the intro can push everything back by up to 2 seconds). The director moves the camera and puts the caption on screen; you talk over it. Numbers in [brackets] are read off the screen in the live run. Never say one in advance.

If a caption on screen differs from the line below (the fallback lines in `demo/claims-to-proof.md`), read what the screen says.

Pace: about 150 words a minute. Pause on every number.

---

**0:00 · intro** (fly-in from Ethiopia to Hawassa)

Speaker A: Food meant for starving children is being stolen and sold in markets. Most of it is taken by people inside the supply chain.

**0:13 · map**

Speaker B: This is Hawassa in southern Ethiopia, with real roads and satellite imagery. The deliveries you're watching are a simulation.

**0:21 · split**

Speaker B: On the left is how aid moves today. On the right, the same requests run on Runners.

**0:29 · clipboard** (card on the left)

Speaker A: Today the paperwork on this van shows where every crate is going and where all [3] stores are.

**0:38 · theft**

Speaker A: An insider takes 1.2 kilos from a crate. We run the same theft on both sides.

**0:46 · today-blind**

Speaker A: On the left nobody gets an alert. That row stays at [0]. The loss waits for a stock count, with no name on it.

**0:55 · black-box** (camera on the Runners vehicle)

Speaker B: On the right, every vehicle carries a black box. It tells the crew the next step, and the crates stay sealed.

**1:04 · crew**

Speaker B: The crew never writes down what's inside or who it's for. The black box keeps that.

**1:12 · handoff** (wait for the caption to change)

Speaker B: Now watch the next hand-off. The receiving vehicle weighs the crate on the spot.

Speaker B (when the Leak Finder lights up): There. Crate [p-31] is [1.2] kilos light. The Leak Finder names [moto-04] and the cell it was in.

**1:30 · breach-today**

Speaker A: Now someone steals the van's phone. On the left it gives away [3] store locations and [N] notes on children.

**1:38 · breach-runners**

Speaker A: Steal a Runners black box and you get [0] store locations and [0] notes on children. It only knows its next stop.

**1:47 · network-down**

Speaker B: Now the towers go down on both sides. This is the Neptune North brief: keep working when communication fails.

**1:56 · swarm**

Speaker B: The drones relay messages between vehicles. You can see [N] links running through them.

**2:05 · outage**

Speaker A: Since the outage, Runners delivered [N] crates. Today's dispatch delivered [N].

**2:14 · network-up**

Speaker B: Network back. The receipts each vehicle carried merge into one ledger.

**2:19 · ledger**

Speaker B: [N] crates on the ledger. [N] delivered intact, and [N] losses traced to a named hand-off.

**2:27 · glass-box**

Speaker A: Auditors get a glass box. This is that crate's chain of custody, with the weight at every hand-off. The drop sits with [moto-04].

**2:37 · scoreboard**

Speaker B: Same requests and same events on both sides. Today delivered [N] intact and named [0] losses. Runners delivered [N] and named [N].

**2:46 · close**

Speaker A: Every sachet reaches a clinic, or the ledger names the hand-off where it went missing.

**2:52 · title**

Speaker A: That's Runners. Thanks for watching.

## Slide facts

The demo needs no slides. If you add a title slide or a judge asks for a source, use only these (PLAN section 9), with the source on the slide:

- RUTF meant for malnourished children has been found stolen and sold in markets in north-east Nigeria. Source: HumAngle; Daily Trust.
- MSF's research unit found that full cartons at resale sites were most likely diverted by people inside the health system, NGOs or UN agencies. Source: MSF CRASH.
- In 2023, USAID and WFP suspended food aid across Ethiopia over a diversion scheme that officials said may have been the largest theft of food aid in history, including manipulated beneficiary lists. Source: PBS/AP; WFP statement.
- In January 2022, a cyber attack on the ICRC compromised data on more than 515,000 highly vulnerable people. Source: ICRC.

Don't put any other number on a slide. The Hawassa scenario is simulated, so don't suggest that any real clinic or store in Hawassa has been robbed.
