## Gaps to close before recording

- **Black box and glass box modules.** The director calls `showBlackBox` / `showGlassBox` from `src/ui/blackbox.ts` and `src/ui/glassbox.ts` if they exist. Until then, claims 7, 8 and 18 rest on the inspector and the demo's chain card.
- **Claim 11 timing.** The skim target is a loaded vehicle that is already in radio range of another vehicle or a clinic, and the sim runs at 40x during the wait. Check in a rehearsal that the Leak Finder fires before 1:30 on the locked seed.
- **Claim 15.** Drones relay because the mesh forwards through any node in range. If the agent team adds a dedicated relay behaviour, the caption still holds; if a rehearsal shows 0 drone links, use the fallback line.
- **Claim 17.** "Hop by hop" is design, not something the screen animates. Keep the script on "merge".
