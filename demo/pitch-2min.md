# Runners: two-minute voiceover

One speaker. Read at about 140 words per minute, leaving space for the evidence.
The video is a simulation on real geography. Numbers remain on screen; the narration does not assume a particular delivery count.
Timing cues below follow the planned 118-second edit and will be checked against the final cut.

## 0:00-0:14 | The problem and product

Runners lets delivery fleets coordinate without a central connection.

When phone coverage disappears, clinics still need their supplies. Our prototype simulates twenty-nine vehicles on the real roads of Hawassa, Ethiopia.

## 0:14-0:21 | Central dispatch

On the left, central dispatch holds the common picture. On the right, Runners distributes decisions across the fleet.

## 0:21-0:29 | Introduce the incident

We inject the same theft into both simulations. An insider removes food from a crate. Now the system has to identify where it happened.

## 0:29-0:44 | Local decisions

Each vehicle has an autonomous black box agent. It decides where to move, which neighbour can deliver sooner, and when to transfer a crate. The crew receives the next instruction. The contents remain sealed.

## 0:44-0:55 | Loss detection

Every crate carries a signed custody passport. The next receiver weighs it, detects the missing weight and names the previous holder. Detection happens locally.

## 0:55-1:12 | Device privacy

Now we breach the same last-mile device in both systems. Central dispatch exposes store locations and clinic notes. The Runners device holds routing information without those sensitive records. Access is restricted by role.

## 1:12-1:32 | Offline coordination

Now the towers go down. Nearby Runners agents communicate over local radio. Where no connection exists, vehicles carry requests and receipts until they can forward them. The fleet keeps making decisions without a central dispatcher. Watch the delivery comparison.

## 1:32-1:47 | Reconnection and audit

When connectivity returns, signed records merge. An authorised auditor can open the manifest and inspect the custody history, including the exact weight change. Nothing is foolproof; we make misuse detectable and attributable.

## 1:47-1:58 | Results and ambition

We measure delivered supplies, detected losses and exposed data. Food delivery is our starting point. The same architecture could support medicine distribution, disaster response and remote field operations.

Runners keeps coordination in the field.
